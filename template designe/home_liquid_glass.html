The Studio

PLAYING

Vakar Games is an independent studio. We focus on a small number of projects at a time — not because we have to, but because we believe constraint leads to better work.

We develop across PC and mobile, with a focus on tight mechanics, clear design and experiences that respect the player's time.

Our toolset is built in-house: we manage projects, players, missions and shop systems from our own platform, so we can move fast without losing control.

01
Game Development
From first prototype to final release. We work across PC and mobile with a focus on gameplay that feels right, not just looks right.
02
Community & Players
Players are part of the process. We share progress, collect feedback and iterate — because better games come from that loop.
03
Internal Tooling
We build what we need. Our studio platform manages accounts, game data, shop systems and team workflows — all under our control.
